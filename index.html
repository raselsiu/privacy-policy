Last updated: September 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how “বর্ণমালা ও সংখ্যা পরিচয়” (“the App”) handles information when you use our application.

The App is an educational application designed primarily for children to learn Bengali letters, numbers, basic concepts, and other educational content in a simple and enjoyable way.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting children and users.

The App does not require users to create an account or provide personal information to use its main educational features.

We do not directly collect or store personal information such as:

  • Full name

  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Passwords
  • Contacts
  • Photos or videos
  • Precise GPS location
  • Financial or payment information

However, the App may use third-party services, such as Google AdMob, which may automatically process certain technical and advertising-related information when advertisements are displayed or requested.

Depending on the services and settings used, this may include:

  • Device and app information
  • IP address
  • Advertising or app-related identifiers, where permitted

  • Approximate location derived from IP address
  • Diagnostic, performance, and security information

We do not intentionally request personal information directly from children.

2. Children's Privacy

The App is primarily intended for children and families.

We take children's privacy seriously and design the App to comply with applicable children's privacy and advertising requirements, including the Google Play Families Policy and applicable AdMob policies.

We do not knowingly ask children to provide personal information such as their name, address, phone number, email address, or precise location.

Where advertising is used, advertising services are configured for child-directed use in accordance with applicable policies.

We do not knowingly use personalized or interest-based advertising, remarketing, or similar advertising practices directed at children.
